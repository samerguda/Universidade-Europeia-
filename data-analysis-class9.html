  • Theory Behind Clustering Methods
    • Similarity Measures:
      • Euclidean Distance: Measures the straight-line distance between points.
      • Manhattan Distance: Calculates the sum of absolute differences between points.
    • Clustering Criteria:
      • Intra-cluster Variation: Maximize similarity within clusters to ensure that data points are closely related.
      • Inter-cluster Variation: Maximize differences between clusters to ensure that groups are sufficiently distinct.
